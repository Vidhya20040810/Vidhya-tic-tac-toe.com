About Tic Tac Toe Extended

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe Extended! We offer a fresh twist on the classic game you know and love. While you're familiar with the traditional 3x3 grid, we've expanded the possibilities with larger game boards for a more strategic and engaging experience.

Our Unique Game Variations

  • 5x5 Tic Tac Toe

    On our 5x5 grid, the goal remains the same: be the first player to get a line of your symbols horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. However, in this variation, you need to get three of your symbols in a row to win. This adds a new layer of tactical depth, requiring you to think several steps ahead and control more space on the board.

  • 6x6 Tic Tac Toe

    For an even greater challenge, try our 6x6 grid! Similar to the 5x5 version, the winning condition is to achieve a line of three of your symbols in a row. The larger board size opens up even more strategic opportunities and makes each game a unique puzzle to solve.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a fun and engaging way to play Tic Tac Toe with friends and family. By introducing these larger board variations with the familiar "three-in-a-row" win condition, we aim to offer a blend of nostalgic gameplay with a refreshing strategic depth that keeps players coming back for more.

We believe that these extended versions of Tic Tac Toe offer a more nuanced and satisfying experience, appealing to both casual players and those looking for a bit more of a strategic challenge.
